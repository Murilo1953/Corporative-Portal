AFS-FAC-"CAT 3 DUAL" message is shown on the "INOP SYS" section of the SD without other ECAM message related to the FAC.

Affected computerFAC
Reset Procedure
If the "CAT 3 DUAL" message is shown on the "INOP SYS" section of the SD without other ECAM message(s):
-
Change the AP in command.
If not satisfactory:
-
Release the FAC 1 pushbutton switch (the OFF legend comes on).
-
Wait for three seconds.
-
Push the FAC 1 pushbutton switch (the OFF legend goes off).
Wait for the "AUTO FLT FAC 1 FAULT" message to go out of view:
-
Release the FAC 2 pushbutton switch (the OFF legend comes on).
-
Wait for three seconds.
-
Push the FAC 2 pushbutton switch (the OFF legend goes off).
NOTE:
If the "CAT 3 DUAL" message (on the "INOP SYS" section of the SD) is related to an "ADR" or "IR" ECAM message, the FAC or the FMGC do not see the ADR or the IR as defective. Thus, a change of the AP or a reset of the FAC will not clear the "CAT 3 DUAL" message.
