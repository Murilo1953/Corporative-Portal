Section 1

Wing-to-Fuselage Fairings & Belly-Fairing Access Panels

AMM reference: TASK 53-35-00-200-002-A for missing fasteners.
Panel 198DB: missing or damaged fasteners are not permitted.

Temporary replacement

A missing fastener may be temporarily replaced by a fastener from another panel, provided the thread has the same dimension.

Maintenance focus

Forward edges, corners and service-panel areas often have stricter limits than remaining panel edges.

Section 2

Pylon Fairing Panels

Forward edge

Missing fasteners are not permitted.

Corners

Missing fasteners are not permitted.

Allowable quantity

One missing fastener per panel is permitted for a maximum of 10 FC.

SRM

Refer to 54-52-11-283-014 — ADL for Missing Fasteners.

A320 Pylon Fairing Panels
Section 3

Vertical Stabilizer Trailing Edge Access Panels

Only panels 325BL, 325BR, 325CL, 325CR and 325DL have fasteners.

General allowance

One missing fastener is permitted in each panel.

Temporary transfer

A fastener may be temporarily taken from another panel if the replacement has the same size and length.

Donor-panel limit

325BL/BR/CL/CR: maximum 4 missing fasteners per panel. 325DL: maximum 5.

Rectification

Install missing fasteners within 100 FH or less.

Panels 325BL, 325BR, 325CL, 325CR and 325DL
Section 4

Vertical Stabilizer Fairings & Rudder Fairings

Panels: 323AL/AR & 325AL/AR

ZONE 1

Missing fasteners are not permitted.

ZONE 2

Maximum two missing fasteners, not adjacent. Each missing position must be bounded by two correctly installed adjacent fasteners.

Rectification

Replace missing fasteners before 100 flight cycles.

Panels 323AL/AR & 325AL/AR
