Flight Phases 1 to 10 — Visual Technical Guide

The A320 Flight Warning Computers determine ten operational flight phases from aircraft configuration signals. The phases are used by the warning/display logic and are independent from FMGC flight phases.

FWC 1 / FWC 2 ECAM T.O. INHIBIT LDG INHIBIT FCOM DSC-31-15
Warning inhibition: T.O. INHIBIT is associated with phases 3–5, while LDG INHIBIT is associated with phases 7–8.

FWC logic

The 10 Flight Phases

1

Flight Phase 1

Electrical power ON → first engine start

On ground; both engines stopped.

2

Flight Phase 2

First engine start → takeoff power

On ground; below 80 kt; at least one engine running.

3

Flight Phase 3

Takeoff power applied → accelerating to 80 kt

On ground; below 80 kt; takeoff power set.

4

Flight Phase 4

80 kt → liftoff

On ground; above 80 kt; takeoff power set.

5

Flight Phase 5

Liftoff → 1500 ft

Airborne; RA below 1500 ft. Maximum duration: 2 min.

6

Flight Phase 6

1500 ft → 800 ft

Airborne cruise/climb segment outside phases 5 and 7.

7

Flight Phase 7

Below 800 ft → touchdown

Approach/descent logic. Maximum duration: 3 min.

8

Flight Phase 8

Touchdown → decelerating to 80 kt

On ground; takeoff power not set.

9

Flight Phase 9

Below 80 kt → second engine shutdown

On ground; at least one engine running.

10

Flight Phase 10

Second engine shutdown → 5 min later

On ground; both engines stopped; then returns to phase 1.

Technical notes

FWC Notes

Phase 5: if the aircraft remains airborne beyond the phase-5 time limit, the logic transitions to phase 6.
Phase 7: approach logic remains active until ground detection or climb above the applicable altitude threshold; its duration is limited.
Ground logic: the source describes “on ground” using landing-gear downlocked/compressed information together with very low radio-altimeter height.
Reference: detailed flight-phase logic is identified in the source as FCOM DSC-31-15.
